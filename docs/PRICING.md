# Pricing & Packaging

MABRIG DevShield AI follows an **open-core + managed security** model.

The self-managed GitHub Action and local CLI remain free. Paid value is attached to implementation, managed review, centralized governance, analytics and support.

**Sales:** mabrig@mabrigkorie.org

## Packaging principle

The free product should remain genuinely useful. Paid plans should sell **convenience, context, governance and outcomes**, not artificially cripple the open scanner.
