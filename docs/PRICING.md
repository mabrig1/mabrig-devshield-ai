# Pricing & Packaging

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Included reviews | Best for |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 20 deterministic | Open-source evaluation and small repos |
| Starter | $9 | $90 | 200 managed | Solo developers and indie SaaS |
| Pro | $29 | $290 | 1,000 managed | Growing products and agencies |
| Team | $79 | $790 | 5,000 managed | Multi-repository engineering teams |

## Upsell logic

**Free → Starter:** managed AI review, higher review allowance.

**Starter → Pro:** larger allowance, deeper review prompts, future policy presets and analytics.

**Pro → Team:** organization-wide usage, centralized rules, future dashboard/export and priority support.

GitHub Marketplace paid plans for the companion GitHub App should be configured in USD with the billing intervals supported by GitHub at submission time. Reconfirm Marketplace pricing requirements before the commercial App listing is submitted.
